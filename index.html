Barry's Big Goal

Big dreams start with belief.

Meet Barry, a young baller who loves the game—but when he doesn’t make the team, he discovers that greatness is more than talent. It’s heart, resilience, and the courage to keep going.

From educator and storyteller JB Rascal, this inspiring picture book is for every kid chasing a dream—on or off the field.
